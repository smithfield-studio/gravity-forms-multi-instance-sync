# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Each Gravity Form renders once per page and moves between its placements: into a placement as it nears the viewport, or as soon as it's revealed (e.g. a modal opening), keeping the visitor's answers and step.
- A form in a hidden placement moves to a visible one as the page is parsed, ahead of WP Rocket's Delay JS.
- Empty placements show a link to the form, from a template a theme can override, translated for 12 locales.
